John Angelson Arellano — known as JAS — is a photographer and art director based in Quezon City, Metro Manila. From mountain summits to ballroom floors, he documents the world with precision and restraint.

Whether setting up an intricate commercial flatlay for a brand's product launch, or catching the unguarded joy of an 18th debut cotillion — every frame is shot with intention and meticulous control of light.
